# Examples

## Hotel rate inquiry

Purpose: Ask a hotel about availability and total rates for specified dates and guests.

Allowed: Collect availability, room type, rate, taxes, cancellation terms, and contact instructions.

Not allowed: Book, pay, or claim a reservation exists without user confirmation and a supported booking result.

## Service vendor inquiry

Purpose: Ask an authorized repair or service provider about availability, estimated arrival time, and quoted cost.

Allowed: Capture the provider's stated availability, ETA, and quote.

Not allowed: Accept the quote or authorize work without explicit user approval.

## Appointment availability inquiry

Purpose: Ask a business for available appointment windows.

Allowed: Collect available dates, times, prerequisites, and contact instructions.

Not allowed: Present an appointment as booked unless the call explicitly confirms booking and the user authorized that action.
